2. Verify the attorney and the LOP

Lien reconciliation and release desk for an injury practice

Every settled injury lien checked against what was actually paid, with short payments flagged before any lien release goes out.

  • Track every open lien with its attorney, lien holder, billed total, payments posted and balance, from treatment through settlement and release
  • Flag settled liens where the payment, the attorney or the lien holder does not match the ledger, with the days left before the attorney's disbursement deadline
  • Attach the attorney's settlement or reduction letter to the flagged lien and send it to release review
  • Approve each reduction and lien release before the release letter goes to the law firm

4. Records requests and releases

Records request and release desk for an injury practice

Every law firm records and bill request matched to the right chart and checked before anything is released.

  • Track every records and itemized bill request from law firms, from arrival through release, with the channel it came in on and the firm's due date
  • Match each request to the right chart and check the patient's authorization for signature, expiry, birth date and date range, flagging any mismatch with the days left before the firm's due date
  • Attach a corrected authorization to the flagged request and send it to release review
  • Approve each records packet, with the itemized bill and final balance taken from the ledger, before it is released to the firm

7. Itemized bills and ledgers

PIP claim and denial tracker for an injury practice

Every PIP claim tracked from submission to payment, with denials sorted by reason and appeal deadlines counted down.

  • Track every PIP claim from submission through payment, denial or handoff, with the carrier, adjuster and benefits used so far
  • Sort each carrier EOB into paid, underpaid, denied, records requested or benefits exhausted, and flag exhaustion notices that do not match the carrier's own payment history
  • Count down appeal and timely filing deadlines on every open denial
  • Approve each reconsideration before it goes to the adjuster, or hand a truly exhausted balance to the LOP coordinator with the full claim history
